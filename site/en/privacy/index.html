Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Minik Kaşif uses and how.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information from you or your child and sends nothing to our servers. No account is needed.

2. Information stored on the device

Profile name, age, chosen avatar, earned stars and stickers, drawings and settings are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app. You can reset progress at any time in the Parents Area.

3. Ads and tracking

The app contains no ads, no advertising identifiers and no behavioural tracking.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only learn from the store whether your subscription is active.

5. Voice

Sentences without a recording are read by your device's text-to-speech feature, which depends on your device settings.

6. Children's privacy

The app is made for children. Purchase and settings screens are protected by a parent gate.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app. Questions: destek@minikkasif.app
