Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. After you cancel, the subscription stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. In the app you can also go to Parent Area → Subscription → "Manage or cancel subscription".

My subscription does not show on a new device.

Tap Parent Area → Subscription → "Restore purchases". You must be signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where is my child's progress stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server; if you delete the app, the progress is deleted too.

What is the parent gate?

A short question, easy for an adult, asked before settings, the subscription screen and any outside link.

Does the app need the internet?

No. The games work offline; only subscription actions need an internet connection.
